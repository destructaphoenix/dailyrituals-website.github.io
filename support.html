Support

Contact us

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email us at admin@destructaphoenix.dev. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data backed up?

Your journal lives only on your phone; we have no server that holds it. Two things keep it safe: if Android backup is on, Android copies it to your Google account, and You → Back up my journal saves a file you can keep anywhere you trust.

I switched phones — can I transfer my data?

If Android backup was on, restore your Google backup when you set up the new phone and Daily Rituals comes back with it. Or use You → Back up my journal on the old phone, then You → Restore from a backup on the new one.

How do I reset my data?

Go to You → Reset all data, or uninstall the app. Either permanently deletes the journal from your phone. A copy may remain in your Google backup until Google replaces it.

I was charged but don't have Plus access

Open the Plus screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't resolve it, email us with your Google Play order number and we'll help investigate.

How do I cancel my Plus subscription?

Cancel through the Google Play Store → Subscriptions (not inside the app). You'll keep Plus access until the end of your current billing period.

I found a bug

Please email admin@destructaphoenix.dev with a description of what happened, your Android version, and steps to reproduce. Screenshots are very helpful.
